Why this exists

Volunteering across Peru—constructing homes, roads, and schools—taught me a powerful lesson: local communities thrive when they have direct access to essential tools and clear information.

Back home in Arizona, that lesson hit close to home on January 1, 2023. When Rio Verde Foothills suddenly lost its primary water supply, families less than an hour away were left scrambling for answers. The information they needed wasn't missing; it was trapped in fragmented government databases designed for analysts, not residents in urgent need.

This platform was created to change that. We bring together scattered datasets to give you fast, transparent, and easy-to-understand answers. Starting with immediate water access points, we break down everything from supplier details and safety records to underlying regulatory trends.

Knowledge is the best tool for water security. By making vital data accessible to everyone today, we can help Arizona communities stay proactive, informed, and resilient for tomorrow.

Sidak Mann, Arizona
