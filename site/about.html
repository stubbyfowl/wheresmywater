About this directory

Why this exists

On January 1, 2023, Scottsdale cut off the hauled water that hundreds of homes in Rio Verde Foothills depended on. Those residents had no municipal provider, no city to call, and no single place to find out what their options were.

The information that would have helped them existed, but it was scattered: haulers advertised on their own, federal compliance data sat in a database nobody browses, and neighbors ended up building their own website because nothing else covered it.

This site pulls those pieces into one answer. It starts with Rio Verde Foothills because that's where the need was clearest and where real places to get water could be verified by hand.

Know a water source we're missing?

No government agency keeps a list of haulers and standpipes. This directory is built by hand, which means it's incomplete by default. If you know a source that should be here, tell us.
