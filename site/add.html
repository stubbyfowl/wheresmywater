Add or correct a source

No agency keeps a list of Arizona's water haulers and standpipes, so this directory is compiled by hand. If you know a source we're missing, or a listing here is wrong or out of date, telling us is the fastest way it gets fixed.

This opens your email app with the details filled in, so nothing is submitted to a server and you can see exactly what gets sent. If your email app doesn't open, use "Copy instead" and paste it into a message to sidaksmann@gmail.com.

Please read: This is an independent project, not a government service and not affiliated with any water utility. The information here is gathered from public records and research, and it can be out of date or incomplete. Always call a water source before relying on it. In an emergency, contact your county emergency management office.
