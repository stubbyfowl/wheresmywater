    What to do

    My water is out right now
    1. Search your address above and call the haulers listed. Ask about emergency or same-day delivery, and confirm they serve your road, not just your town.
    2. Standpipes usually are not walk-up. EPCOR's Rio Verde Foothills station needs an account and a PIN before you can draw water, so signing up is step one, not step two.
    3. If you cannot reach anyone, call your county emergency management office. They coordinate water assistance during outages and can point to bulk distribution if it exists.
    4. For drinking in the meantime, bottled water from any store is safe. Do not drink from a well or a tank you suspect is contaminated.
    I rely on hauled water
    1. Know your tank size and roughly how many days it lasts. Order before you are near empty, since haulers schedule days ahead and demand spikes in summer.
    2. Ask any hauler whether they carry potable water specifically. Construction water and dust-control water are hauled by the same kind of truck and are not for drinking.
    3. Ask where they source from. A hauler drawing from a permitted public system is drawing water that gets tested.
    4. Keep a second hauler's number. Single-supplier dependence is exactly what went wrong in Rio Verde Foothills in 2023.
    I have a private well
    1. Nobody tests your well for you. Private wells are outside EPA and ADEQ drinking-water rules, so testing is on the owner.
    2. Test at least annually for bacteria and nitrate, and in Arizona also consider arsenic, fluoride and uranium, which occur naturally in some aquifers here. Use an ADHS-licensed lab.
    3. Look up your well in ADWR's registry to confirm depth and construction details before you assume anything about it.
    4. If the water level is dropping, note it. Regional decline shows up in the well and subsidence data this site reports as background context.
    I'm buying property without a water connection
    1. Search the address here first. If no provider serves it, no utility is obligated to bring water to that lot, ever.
    2. Ask specifically whether the parcel has a 100-year assured water supply determination. Lots split without one are how Rio Verde Foothills happened.
    3. Get the well's actual production rate in writing, not the seller's description of it.
    4. Price hauled water into your monthly costs before you buy, not after.
    My tap water looks, smells or tastes wrong
    1. If you have a provider, look up its record above, then request its Consumer Confidence Report. Every community water system must give you one and it lists what was detected.
    2. Report the problem to your provider first and write down when you reported it.
    3. If they do not respond, ADEQ's Safe Drinking Water Program takes complaints about public water systems.
    4. Discoloured water after main work is usually sediment. Persistent taste, smell or illness is worth escalating rather than waiting out.

    This is general guidance assembled from public sources, not legal, medical or engineering advice. When something here conflicts with what your provider, your county, or a licensed professional tells you about your specific situation, believe them, not this page.
